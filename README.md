# 📚 My School Planner

A lightweight, efficient, and customizable daily agenda and class schedule tracker designed to help students keep their classes, club activities, and homework organized.

# ✨ Features

- **Daily Agenda Tracking:** Structure your daily classes and periods seamlessly.
- **Club & Extracurricular Schedule:** Keep track of club meetings, rehearsals, and extracurricular practices alongside your academics.
- **Spreadsheet Integration:** Easy-to-use formulas and layout configured for Google Sheets / Excel workflows.
- **Minimalist & Clean UI:** Designed to reduce clutter and keep focus purely on daily productivity.
