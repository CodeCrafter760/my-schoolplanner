code: 6 features that could make Bibi Plans stand out







1. My School Schedule
Enter your class periods, lunch, and after-school clubs once. Bibi Plans automatically displays your daily schedule and connects assignments to the right classes.










2. Exam Prep Mode
Give each test a date, list the topics, rate your confidence in each topic, and generate a revision checklist for the days before the test.










3. Auto-plan My Day
Choose your available study hours and assignments. The planner suggests when to work on each assignment, starting with urgent tasks and leaving room for breaks.










4. Digital Scrapbook
Add stickers, sticky notes, doodles, and decorative widgets to personalize the dashboard. Users could even create different layouts for each school term.










5. Grade and Goal Tracker
Track assignment scores, calculate averages, and set goals for each subject. Keep grades optional and private.










6. Quick Capture Inbox
Quickly save homework, reminders, or random thoughts without filling out a form. Organize them into the correct class or category later.







What I'd build first
My top three would be:
1. Auto-plan My Day — a genuinely useful feature that differentiates the app from a basic assignment tracker.
2. Exam Prep Mode — perfect for students who have multiple tests coming up.
3. My School Schedule — makes the planner useful every single day.
These features build on established planner patterns like unified calendars, study timers, recurring events, and project checklists.
